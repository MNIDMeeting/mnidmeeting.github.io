Lower-cost campus housing is available to MNID 2026 attendees through the University of Wisconsin–Madison StarRez portal. Follow the steps below to book. Click any screenshot to view it at full size.

<div class="housing-steps" markdown="1">

## Step 1

Go to the online registration portal:

[uwmadison.starrezhousing.com/StarRezPortalConference/Login](https://uwmadison.starrezhousing.com/StarRezPortalConference/Login)

## Step 2

Conference participants will **Register here** where it is circled in yellow below. (If you already have an account from a prior stay, use **Log in** with your existing email and password.)

<a href="#img-step2" class="housing-step__figure">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/UniversityHousing/Step2.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Step 2 — Register here on the StarRez portal login page">
</a>

## Step 3

Once logged in, you'll see your name at the top of the page. Click **Summer Guests**.

<a href="#img-step3" class="housing-step__figure">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/UniversityHousing/Step3.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Step 3 — Summer Guests option on the account page">
</a>

## Step 4

Click **Continue** at the bottom of the page.

<a href="#img-step4" class="housing-step__figure">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/UniversityHousing/Step4.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Step 4 — Continue button at the bottom of the page">
</a>

## Step 5

Choose **Select** for your corresponding conference or program.

<a href="#img-step5" class="housing-step__figure">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/UniversityHousing/Step5.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Step 5 — Select the corresponding conference or program">
</a>

## Step 6

Once you select the conference group, two pages of overview and questions will follow. Housing options appear on the next page, where you can adjust your dates (keep the full range of offered nights, or arrive later and/or depart earlier) and choose a spot in a Double Room or a Single Room, depending on what the conference is offering.

Please note:

- A **Single** room means you are paying to be alone in a room, to yourself.
- If you choose a **Double** room, you pay for one spot in a double room for two people. If you have a roommate preference, indicate that — your roommate must also choose you in order to be placed together. If you do not make a roommate choice, you will be paired with another attendee of the same identifying gender from your conference.

<a href="#img-step6" class="housing-step__figure">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/UniversityHousing/Step6.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Step 6 — Housing options and room selection page">
</a>
